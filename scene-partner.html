What formats work?
  • NAME: line — HAMLET: To be, or not to be
  • NAME. line — the style used by most free Shakespeare texts
  • Screenplay style — the character name in CAPS on its own line, with their dialogue underneath
  • Anything in (parentheses) or [brackets] is a stage direction and won't be read as dialogue.
